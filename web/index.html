A comparison of fixed and adaptive traffic lights.

Settings

Arrival probability is the chance of a car arriving each second (0 to 1). Use 0.65 for heavy traffic. The same seed repeats the same arrivals.

Timing rule: Both lights use 30 seconds of red. The adaptive light adds 10 seconds of green if more than 10 cars are waiting when green begins.
